---
description: Check whether new work is in-scope or out-of-scope for the current mission
---

Compare the proposed work against the current mission's stated objective (from `PROJECT_LOG.md` or the active brief).

- In scope → proceed.
- Out of scope → do NOT silently expand. Log it under `[PENDING SCOPE]` in `PROJECT_LOG.md` as secondary/tertiary (mission queue rules in `QUICK_REFERENCE.md`), and ask the human whether to include it now or later.
